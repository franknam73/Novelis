~에 대응하여 (반응/조치)		 Due to identifies a cause; in response to identifies an action or reaction taken afterward. "Due to port congestion, we adjusted dispatch schedules in response to client inventory alerts."
In line with
~와 일치하여 / 부합하여		 In proportion to
~에 비례하여		 In line with means consistent with a plan/target; in proportion to expresses a relative mathematical ratio. "Recycled content is in line with our 2027 target, and rebates scale in proportion to scrap return volume."
Subject to
~을 조건으로 / 승인 하에		 Dependent on
~에 달려 있는 / 의존하는		 Both express conditions, but subject to is standard in formal contracts, legal clauses, and executive approvals. "The revised Q1 alloy pricing is subject to regional VP approval and dependent on LME aluminum benchmarks."
Prior to
~ 이전에 (시점)		 In advance of
~에 앞서 (사전 준비 강조)		 Both mean before; in advance of emphasizes proactive preparation ahead of an upcoming milestone. "Please circulate the tariff impact memo in advance of Friday's review, and confirm figures prior to noon."

Part 4. Action Speech & Logical Executive Reporting (45 mins)

Delivering structured, 2-minute executive briefings using the Power Position, ATEC Formula, and precise data commentary.

02:00
The ATEC Executive Briefing Formula & Data Commentary Stand in the Power Position • Eye contact • Pause before key numbers
A Attention (Hook & Bottom-Line Up Front)
Capture executive focus immediately with the core commercial or operational headline.
"With respect to Q1 automotive sheet demand, we are seeing an accelerated shift toward high-recycled-content alloys."
T Topic (Scope & Strategic Framing)
Define the exact scope of your update.
"Today, I want to walk you through three key drivers impacting our margins and delivery timeline."
E Evidence / Experience (Data & Root Cause)
Substantiate with metrics, trends, and cause-and-effect prepositions.
"Shipments rose substantially by 12% in Q3, owing to stronger EV platform orders, though freight costs climbed marginally."
C Closing (Recommendation & Next Step)
End decisively with a clear ask or decision.
"On the basis of these figures, I recommend locking in Q1 rolling capacity prior to December 15."
Data & Trend Phrases (Sec. 4): Increases: a substantial increase in / an upward trend in / reached a peak in / projected to soar  |  Decreases: just under target / way below expectations / downturn began in / continued to fall  |  Fluctuations: steady progress / slight fluctuations / remained volatile
